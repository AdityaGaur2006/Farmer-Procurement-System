COMMON QUESTIONS

How can we help?

How does my token number work?

Your token protects your queue position. Arrive about 10 minutes before the slot; you will be checked in at the centre.

Why did the recommended mandi change?

Mandi Mitra considers current recorded rates, distance, transport estimate and queue information every time you request a comparison.

When will I receive payment?

Once the accepted quantity and quality report are finalised, payment status appears in the Payments page.

How can I report a problem?

Call the helpline with your farmer ID and booking/token number. An operator can also assist you at the procurement centre.
